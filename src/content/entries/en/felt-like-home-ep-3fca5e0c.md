Felt Like Home is an EP written, produced and released by a group of eight students on my course, out on 24 April 2026.

I coordinated the project from the first writing sessions to the release, mixed and mastered every track so the EP holds together as one record, and wrote and produced two of the tracks, Fl0at and where?, on my own.
