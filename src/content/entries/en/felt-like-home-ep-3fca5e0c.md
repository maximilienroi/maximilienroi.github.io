Felt Like Home is an EP made by a collective of eight students on my course and released on 24 April 2026. Each track looks at the same childhood feeling through a different style.

I coordinated the project from the first writing sessions to the release, and mixed and mastered every track so the EP holds together as one record. I also wrote and produced two of the tracks, Fl0at and where?.
