Felt Like Home is an EP made by a collective of eight students on my course and released on 24 April 2026. Each track looks at the same childhood feeling through a different style.

I coordinated the project from the first writing sessions to the release, mixed and mastered every track so the EP holds together as one record, and looked after the project's social media. I also wrote and produced two of the tracks, Fl0at and where?.
