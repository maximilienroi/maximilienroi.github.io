In my Music Marketing module, the class is taking part in an experimental project: we choose a Year 3 artist and try to push one of their tracks into the UK Top 40 together. Theo and I were asked to lead it, so I set up the project's group chat, which has 19 members, and I help coordinate the artist selection and the campaign.
