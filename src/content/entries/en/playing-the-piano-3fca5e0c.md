I started lessons as a child with private teachers in France and played in the Steinway piano competitions in Paris. I've kept playing on and off ever since, for about 14 years now.

These days I'm mostly interested in jazz: I spend my time on harmony, improvising, learning standards and composing my own pieces, which often end up as tracks or as videos.
