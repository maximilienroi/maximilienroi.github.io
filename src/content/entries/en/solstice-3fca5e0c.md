A group project in which we plan and put on a live indie-electronic show from scratch, to learn how live music is actually organised: finding and booking a venue, the technical setup, the running order and the promotion.

I'm the keyboard player and one of the composers, and I also help with the coordination and the artistic direction of the show. We have our venue now, Area 51 at the university, and the show takes place there on 7 December. For our staging presentation I built a 3D model of the stage in Blender, with the lighting moving from orange to purple to blue, so the whole group could see what the show could look like.
