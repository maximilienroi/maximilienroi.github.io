To keep track of university, music and the society, I built a personal system that holds my whole working life in one place. Notion is the source of truth: linked databases for my modules, projects, people, deadlines and ideas. It's mirrored into Obsidian as a graph, so I can see how everything connects, and my files live in Google Drive.

I update it every day by talking to Claude, which files my notes, keeps my to-do list in order and keeps this website up to date. It's also how I've learnt to use AI properly in my work: the Musician Directory and this site were both built this way.
