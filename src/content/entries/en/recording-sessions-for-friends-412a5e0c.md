I've started using the university studios to record other people. At the start of November I recorded improvised piano on the grand piano in one of the live rooms to accompany a friend reading his own English poetry, which I'd like to turn into short videos. A few days later I spent about three hours at the Harrow campus recording another friend and working on the mix, preparing the session and running it from start to finish as both producer and sound engineer.
