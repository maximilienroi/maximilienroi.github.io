perdu (French for lost) is a project I started at home, built on music made by plants. I read about phytoneurology and the work of Jean Thoby, who uses a device called Bamboo to turn the tiny electrical signals of plants into notes. I recorded MIDI from the plants in my living room and built two tracks around it, perdu.000 and perdu.001.

For the sound, I designed synths in Vital and then Serum 2 that wobble between notes, as if they were breathing, and I hid a processed laugh in both tracks as a small signature. I also drew the MAXRØ character and animated him frame by frame in Procreate. I used the project for my first-year Music Artist Identity assessment, and I'd love to take it further one day.
