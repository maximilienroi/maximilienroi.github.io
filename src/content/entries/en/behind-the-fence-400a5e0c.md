An EP inspired by botanica, EDM and indie electronic music. It's about something most of us felt as children: not knowing what was behind the fence at the end of the garden. I build the tracks from nature recordings and ambient sounds, to bring back that nostalgia while keeping the music moving.

Three tracks are ready, and a rough mix of an early version of the title track is on SoundCloud. The release is planned for mid-2027.
