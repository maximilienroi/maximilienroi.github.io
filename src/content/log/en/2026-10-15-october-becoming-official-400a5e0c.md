The Students' Union accepted our affiliation, and the society became official once the committee had completed its GDPR and committee training.

During the month I launched the Musician Directory, brought graphic design and photography students into the team to build the society's visual identity, and helped at The Session with hosting, lights and sound engineering. On 21 October I was on the other side of the desk for once, playing piano at The Session on a first-year artist's original song. Zoya, our graphic designer, made the logos for our group chats and is now working on posters for The Session and for the society. By the end of the month, the community had grown to around 200 students.
