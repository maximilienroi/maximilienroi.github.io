Jasper Hazel and I started the society to connect musicians across years and courses. In the first month we put up posters, handed out flyers and presented the society in first- and second-year classes, and around 100 students joined the community in the first week.

I also co-hosted the first Session of the year, and since then I've been the back-up host whenever Gracie and Theo, who usually host it, aren't available.
