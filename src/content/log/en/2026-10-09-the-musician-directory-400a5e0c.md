A way for members to find each other. Musicians fill in a Google Form with their instruments, styles and what they're looking for. The answers land in a Google Sheet, and a search page I built with Google Apps Script turns each row into a profile, so anyone can find a singer, a drummer or a producer and get in touch.

50 members joined it in the first month. It's only shared inside the society, to protect members' data, so everyone's details are blurred in this screenshot.
