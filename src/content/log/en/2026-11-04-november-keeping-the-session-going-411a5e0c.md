On 4 November I played at The Session myself, performing two songs with a friend, and the society's final posters came back from Zoya, our graphic designer, ready to be printed.

In November I also became the society's social media manager. I set up its Instagram account, @uow_musicsociety, wrote the bio and put together the first carousel posts and stories with Claude, using Zoya's posters and a student photographer's pictures of The Session. The first post went live on 10 November, and the posts are now published automatically through the official Instagram API with a system I set up with Claude.
