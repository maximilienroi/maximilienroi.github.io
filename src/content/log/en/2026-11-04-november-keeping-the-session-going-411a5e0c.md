On 4 November I played at The Session myself, performing two songs with a friend, and I played there again on 18 November with a small band of fellow students. The society's final posters also came back from Zoya, our graphic designer, ready to be printed.

In November I also became the society's social media manager. I set up its Instagram account, @uow_musicsociety, wrote the bio and put together the first carousel posts and stories with Claude, using Zoya's posters and a student photographer's pictures of The Session. The first post went live on 10 November, and the posts are now published automatically through the official Instagram API with a system I set up with Claude.

On 19 November a few of us from the society were given free tickets to Ditto X: Meet the Music Industry at the O2, so I went with Gracie to hear talks on streaming and vocal production, watch a live set and meet other artists, and I was interviewed there by un:hurd music.
